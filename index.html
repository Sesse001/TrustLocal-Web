Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about TrustLocal.

Is TrustLocal free to use? +
Yes! TrustLocal is completely free for customers. Providers can join and start offering services without any fees.
How do I become a provider? +
Download the app, tap "I'm a Freelancer" during signup, fill in your business details, and our admin team will review your application within 24-48 hours.
How does booking work? +
Browse providers, tap "Request Booking", select a date and time, add a job description, and your provider will confirm or reschedule.
Is my payment secure? +
Currently, payments are arranged directly between customer and provider after service completion. In-app secure payments are coming soon!
How are providers verified? +
Every provider must submit ID verification and their application is manually reviewed by our admin team before approval.
